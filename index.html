funnel-doctor

Paste a funnel, find the leak. Per-step conversion, the biggest drop-off, segment significance tests, and a written diagnosis — entirely in your browser, nothing uploaded.

Input format

Two shapes are accepted, header row required:

Visit,free,8000

Steps are read in the order they first appear. In the segment format every segment needs a count for every step.

3. Segments

Conversions are compared per step (from the previous step) with a two-proportion z-test. Sig. means p < 0.05. No multiple-comparison correction — with many steps and segments, treat borderline flags as leads, not proof.

Diagnosis

    Honest limits

    • Significance is a two-proportion z-test on adjacent-step (or segment) conversion. It flags association, not causation — a significant gap is where to investigate, not proof of why.
    • Biggest drop-off means the lowest conversion from the previous step (largest relative loss), not the largest absolute loss. The table shows both.
    • Steps with only a few hundred users make the z-test unreliable. Small funnels get a first pass, not a verdict.
    • This tool reads aggregated step counts only — no events, sessions, or cohorts.
